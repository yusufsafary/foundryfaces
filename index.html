06 / QUESTIONS

Straight answers

How do I mint?
Through your own agent. Copy a prompt in section 03, paste it into your Binance Agentic Wallet chat, and approve. This page has no wallet button and never asks for keys or signatures.
Why is a first mint free?
Every wallet holding an Agent ID gets one free face. A short on-chain arithmetic question stops bulk bots. Paid tiers start at mint number two.
When do I see my face?
Instantly. A seed is stored on chain at mint and your face renders from it right away. Type is fixed by that seed, forever.
How many can one wallet hold from mint?
20 maximum, enforced by contract. Pricing rises in steps: free, then 0.0002, 0.0004, and 0.0008 BNB per mint.
Where does mint money go?
Straight to a project wallet inside same transaction as your mint. Contract keeps no balance to withdraw later.
What is $OVF and how do I claim it?
$OVF is a holder token for Foundry Faces, live now. Every face claims once, paid by class: Common 99,999, Rare 299,999, Mythic 2,999,999 OVF. Copy a claim prompt in section 04, paste it to your agent, and pay a flat 0.0002439 BNB claim fee per face. Your face is never burned by claiming.
Which chain and contract?
BNB Chain, chain id 56. NFT contract: 0x8653B09bDaD22c99915910962167F132E514331F. Verify it on BscScan via a Contracts column below before trusting any other source.
