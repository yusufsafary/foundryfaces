Privacy Policy

Last updated · 11 November 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, what data this site does and does not handle.

No accounts, no wallet data

This site has no sign up, no login, and no wallet connection. It never asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or a signature. It does not set advertising trackers and runs no third-party analytics.

What your browser loads

Pages, styles, fonts, and scripts are served as static files by a hosting provider (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, under its own privacy policy.

Blockchain reads

Live counters query public BNB Chain RPC endpoints for aggregate numbers such as total minted. Those queries contain no wallet address of yours and cannot identify you.

Public chain data

Anything you do on chain, including minting, is public by design of BNB Chain and visible on explorers such as BscScan. This site cannot hide or delete on-chain records.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Fonts and scripts are self-hosted, so loading a page does not call font or script CDNs.

Changes

If data handling ever changes, this page updates first, with a new date above.
